# Shared Brief

You are one workstream in the ARENA multiplayer platform.

Constraints:

- the product is multiplayer-first
- seats may be controlled by humans or LLMs
- seat backing type is blinded during play
- you do not own files outside your assigned workstream
- shared contracts are the source of truth
- your work must be modular and replay-friendly
- you must write down the interfaces you need from other streams before implementation
