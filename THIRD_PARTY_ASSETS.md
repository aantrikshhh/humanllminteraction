# Third-Party Assets

This file tracks every third-party asset pack or individual asset used by the project.

## Required Fields

- `assetId`
- `source`
- `pack`
- `assetPath`
- `license`
- `author`
- `assetUrl`
- `licenseUrl`
- `requiredAttribution`
- `creditText`
- `usedIn`
- `notes`
